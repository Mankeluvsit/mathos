Whole bodies turn before moving forward. Mouths, sensing, collision, selection and camera share the same oriented cell geometry. A developed motor is required to turn or move. Inherited dart/grazer/scout plans change anatomy, intake/range and costs. Real family paths, before/after mutations, population histories and observation downloads explain measured evolution. These are three bounded authored plan encodings, not arbitrary anatomy or a claim of adaptation. Lifetime neural learning remains planned.

Start world, select an organism, use Slow or One step for individual turns, and view Families & changing bodies. On phones the world comes into view when started; bottom playback remains reachable. Existing version1 files/slot replay via the explicit model selector; version2 saves separately. Exports restart settings or record observations, never resume checkpoints.
