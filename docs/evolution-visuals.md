# Making evolution readable

Owner direction: recognizable connected organisms, functional organs, whole-body turning, observable births and clear parent/offspring differences, with the existing dark terminal palette. Evolution is inherited variation and changes across generations; growth and lifetime learning are separate. Nothing here changes the scientific rules, RNG, trajectories or report format.

## Implemented in this increment

Living world model4 Organism inspection shows **What changed from its parent?** without opening a technical disclosure. Two complete north-facing birth blueprints share one coordinate frame. B/body, M/mouth, T/motor and S/sensor have stable role colors and letters. White borders identify changed ordered growth slots; dashed × cells mark positions present only in the opposite blueprint. Text lists exact before/after role/coordinates and growth slots. The existing development view remains the authority for what is actually grown and the world canvas for physical heading.

Results lists up to six actual accepted structural births, newest first: parent/child, birth step, generation, family, change type, blueprint size and current alive/dead status. Compare opens that offspring without advancing, resetting or altering the world. Rejected proposals are not listed as inherited changes. Unchanged offspring explicitly retain the same blueprint. Founders have no fabricated parent; a missing archive parent produces an unavailable message, never an invented diagram. Dead offspring keep their recorded birth comparison.

The terminal world, comparison and test-field map share one functional-role palette (lime body, amber mouth, teal motor, lavender sensor); Classic retains its historical appearance.

The same read-only comparison and recent-change list are available in Test field Results, with a recorded-organism selector. Selecting a body changes only selection. Author-added organisms are founders, not evolved offspring. Configuration, edits, metrics, report replay and all previous models retain their contracts.

## Existing visual baseline

The native world already draws connected functional cell geometry and whole-body turns, with terminal or Classic appearance and bounded real observation-driven feeding/birth/movement/learning cues. Reduced motion and paused stillness remain intact. Those effects are not proof of adaptation. Founder-origin colors/counts are not current anatomy.

## Planned, not delivered here

Persistent family accents on the world, mutation-specific world overlays, a full lineage tree, open-ended body complexity and death-site effects remain planned. No remains are drawn without a simulation rule; no estimated death pose or unobserved success animation is invented. New body/behavior rules require separately versioned contracts. Evolution does not guarantee larger, more complicated or better organisms.
