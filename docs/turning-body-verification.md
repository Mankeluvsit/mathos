## Limits

Three authored genome-driven layouts and bounded plan mutation are the first body-variation encoding, not arbitrary organ evolution. Cardinal grid pivots are discrete; there is no soft-body mechanics or continuous swimming. Conservative swept rectangles can reject some turns a continuous shape could perform. Fixed ID priority and simple local steering can produce blocking/trapping. Scenarios have fixed food input and a closed world; lifetime training, predation, richer chemistry, general rule editing, checkpoints and autonomous ecology research remain planned. Safari, physical-device performance and full screen-reader conformance remain unverified.
