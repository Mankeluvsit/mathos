# Observable artificial life correction

Owner feedback: the first integrated preview looks like pixels turning into mush and does not convey the intended experience. References: https://thelifeengine.net/ and https://www.thebibites.com/ (including the owner's linked Bibites channel). These are behavioral/product references, not code or asset sources.

Life Engine foregrounds organisms made of functional colored cells, reproduction/competition/evolution and organism/world controls. Bibites foregrounds identifiable gene-dependent creatures, neural behavior, survival and reproduction. MATHOS retains its own pixel-addressable model, mathematics, local-first architecture and both genesis directions.

## Diagnosis

life-reference@1 spreads resource-funded growth into adjacent cells. It has no locomotion, discrete food seeking or functional body parts; similar adjacent colors hide body identity. Passing conservation/replay tests does not establish this product experience. Retain that reference for compatibility; do not pass off a palette change as a solution.

## Corrected first experience

- A quiet dark world, discrete food and separated moving pixel bodies.
- Actual functional cells: body/store, mouth/intake and motor/locomotion. A bounded integer controller senses food and chooses directions; no claim of lifetime learning.
- Seeded small population, limited growth, resource-funded reproduction with space/cooldown, heritable mutation, starvation and age-related death.
- Click or keyboard-select one organism. Show what it is doing, age, energy, parent, generation, traits and the decision equation, all from real state.
- Human-readable population/food/birth/death summaries and recent real events. Detailed accounting is secondary.
- Explicit sunlight input funds replenished food; resource accounting includes that input. Nothing is silently created. Empty primordial mode remains an experimental resource environment without promised spontaneous organisms.
- Default guided ecology link, preserved old reference labs, desktop/mobile views and bounded execution. No imported scripts, copied reference assets, backend or model service.

## Delivery gate

A new, independently versioned ecology model and definition cannot silently change life-reference@1 trajectories/files. Verify food seeking/movement, no overlaps, energy funding, replay, birth/mutation/death, selection and mobile state. Publish a corrected browser preview and source review change; leave source changes unmerged for owner evaluation. Lifetime learning, differentiated development, predation, richer chemistry, multi-scale scheduling and autonomous research remain future work.
